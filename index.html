About Me

Hi! I am a PostDoc at UC Berkeley with Prof. Dawn Song. I received my PhD from the National University of Singapore.

My research focuses on the safety and security of LLMs and LLM agents. Previously, I worked on making AI systems verifiable and auditable through cryptography and watermarking.

I am always open to collaborations. If you share similar interests, feel free to email me.
